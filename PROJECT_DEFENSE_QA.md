# Project Defense Q&A

## Why did you use standardized payment?
Standardized payment is useful because it reduces some geographic payment variation and makes cross-state comparisons more interpretable than raw payment alone.

## Why did you avoid simple averages?
Simple averages can overstate small categories. I used weighted logic based on total services so high-volume categories drive the benchmark appropriately.

## Why focus on Connecticut?
It creates a local benchmark story that is easier to discuss in interviews for Connecticut-based roles while still keeping a national comparison.

## Why separate raw and clean tables?
That makes the workflow reproducible and safer. The raw table preserves the imported file, while the clean table handles typing, validation, and derived metrics.

## How is this useful outside healthcare?
The same workflow applies to claims, operations, finance, pricing, or other large public reporting datasets: import, validate, model, benchmark, and visualize.
