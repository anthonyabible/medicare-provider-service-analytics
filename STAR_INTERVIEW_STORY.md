# STAR Interview Story

## Situation
I wanted a portfolio project that used a real public dataset instead of a small classroom file, and I chose the CMS Medicare Physician & Other Practitioners dataset because it is large, messy enough to require careful cleaning, and relevant to business intelligence and healthcare analytics work.

## Task
My goal was to turn the raw CMS provider-service file into a polished analytics workflow that could answer practical questions about payment drivers, procedure mix, site-of-service differences, and Connecticut versus national performance.

## Action
I created a raw staging table for safe import, built a typed clean table with validation and derived metrics, used weighted payment calculations instead of simple averages, created reporting views for Power BI, and documented the project in a recruiter-friendly GitHub repository with clear business framing.

## Result
The final project demonstrated SQL data cleaning, analytical modeling, benchmarking, and dashboard preparation in a way that is easy for recruiters and hiring managers to review quickly. It also gave me a strong example to discuss how I turn messy data into business-facing insights.
