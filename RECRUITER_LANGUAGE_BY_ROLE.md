# Recruiter Language by Role

## Data Analyst
Built a SQL + Power BI analytics workflow on a large public CMS dataset, creating a cleaned analytical layer, weighted benchmarking metrics, and executive-ready outputs to identify payment drivers, procedure mix, and Connecticut versus national performance.

## Reporting Analyst
Developed a reporting pipeline that transformed raw Medicare provider-service data into validated summary outputs, dashboard-ready views, and state-level benchmarking to support faster insight delivery and cleaner stakeholder communication.

## Business Analyst
Used SQL and Power BI to convert a complex public healthcare dataset into a structured decision-support workflow, highlighting payment concentration, place-of-service differences, and regional benchmark gaps in a way that supports business discussion rather than just data display.

## Operations Analyst
Built a reproducible analytics process that stages, validates, and summarizes a large provider-service dataset to surface high-volume categories, above-baseline combinations, and regional patterns that could inform prioritization and monitoring.
