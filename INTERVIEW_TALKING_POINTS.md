# Interview Talking Points

## Why did you build this?
I wanted a portfolio project that showed more than basic SQL syntax. I picked a large public CMS dataset because it let me demonstrate data cleaning, analytical modeling, weighted benchmarking, and dashboard preparation on a real healthcare dataset.

## What was the hardest part?
The hardest part was making the comparisons meaningful. Instead of relying on simple averages, I used weighted payment logic and standardized-payment metrics so the outputs would be more defensible.

## Why separate raw and clean tables?
It makes the workflow safer and more reproducible. The raw table preserves the import exactly as received, and the clean table handles typing, filtering, and derived metrics for analysis.

## Why Power BI after SQL?
SQL did the heavy data preparation and business logic. Power BI was the presentation layer for KPI cards, state comparisons, provider-type breakdowns, and Connecticut spotlight reporting.

## What should a hiring manager remember?
That I can take messy external data, structure it into a usable model, choose better metrics, and present the results in a way that is easy to explain to a business audience.
